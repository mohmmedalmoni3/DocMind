# Atlas · Beta playbook

This fictional demo playbook describes how the beta program operates.

## Onboarding

Each invited team receives a 30-minute onboarding session with customer success. The team must import at least five documents and ask three questions to complete activation. Customer success collects feedback at the end of the first session and again after seven days.

## Budget

The beta program has a total budget of $48,000. Of this amount, $24,000 is allocated to engineering infrastructure, $14,000 to user research and customer success, and $10,000 to launch communications. The budget owner is Priya Shah. Expenses over $2,000 require approval from Maya Chen.

## Feedback and escalation

Beta participants submit feedback through the workspace feedback form. Critical privacy issues are escalated immediately to Omar Haddad and pause new invitations until resolved. General product feedback is reviewed every Friday. Feature requests are prioritized by impact on activation and answer quality.

## Support

Beta support is available Monday through Friday, from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC. The target first response time is four business hours. Enterprise service-level agreements are not included in the beta program.
