# Project Atlas · Product brief

This is a fictional demo document for DocMind. Atlas is a private knowledge workspace for distributed product teams.

## Mission

Atlas helps teams turn scattered internal documentation into reliable answers. Its primary audience is product managers, customer success teams, and engineers at organizations with 50 to 500 employees. The product promise is simple: less searching, more understanding.

## Launch plan

The Atlas public beta launches on November 18, 2026. The launch owner is Maya Chen. The beta will include 150 invited teams. General availability is planned for February 2027, subject to the beta success criteria. The team will review launch readiness on November 4, 2026.

## Success criteria

The beta succeeds when at least 70% of invited teams activate within the first week, median time to the first useful answer is under 3 minutes, and the weekly active team retention rate exceeds 60% after four weeks. The quality target is 90% grounded answers on the internal evaluation set.

## Product scope

Beta includes document import, semantic and keyword search, cited answers, and workspace-level access controls. Native mobile apps, automated web crawling, and integrations with external CRM systems are out of scope for beta. Offline desktop support is being explored for the next release.

## Team and ownership

Maya Chen owns the product launch. Omar Haddad leads engineering. Sofia Rivera owns design and user research. Priya Shah leads customer success and the beta onboarding program. Engineering has six contributors, design has two, and customer success has three.

## Risks and mitigations

The main launch risks are poor document quality, slow onboarding, and unsupported answers. The mitigations are a pre-import document check, a guided first-run experience, and an evaluation gate requiring 90% grounded answers before release. If the gate fails, the release is delayed rather than lowering the quality bar.
